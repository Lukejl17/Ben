Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026. Repertoire Studio Pty Ltd provides the Ben iOS app.

Home Privacy Terms Support

Local-first

Bills you add (issuer, amount, due date, notes, and photos or PDFs you choose) are stored on your device. Reading a bill happens on the device. We do not operate a server that holds your bills for this version.

Camera and photos

We use the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a bill, and only to read issuer, amount, and due date. You confirm every extract before it is saved. Nothing is saved in silence.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications on your phone. We do not send push from a server.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, payment is processed by Apple. We do not see your card. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase. We may use a purchase SDK to confirm that your subscription is active. That confirmation is not your bill data.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your bills for ads. You pay for Ben, so your data is never the product.

Optional analytics, email-in, and cloud accounts are not part of this version. If we add them later, this policy will change first.

Your copy, your delete

You can delete a bill in the app, or delete the app, which removes local data. Export exists so you can take a copy.

Children

Ben is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Email support@benandbill.app. We are in Australia. You can also contact the OAIC if you have a privacy complaint.

We will update this page if our practices change.
