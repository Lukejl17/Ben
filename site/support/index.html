Subscriptions and refunds

Apple handles payment. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refund requests also go through Apple. We can help you find that path if you email us.

What we cannot see

Ben is local-first. We cannot open the bills on your phone from here. If something looks wrong, tell us what you tapped and what you expected. Screenshots help.
