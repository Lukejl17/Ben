Ben · design direction board 01

Four homes for Ben. Same bills, four personalities.

Every phone below shows the identical home screen — hero bill, monthly summary, category donut, two due sections, dial and tab bar — so the only thing changing is the design language. All four take your three notes: a stronger display font, gradient atmosphere, and widget-first separation.

How to react: mix and match out loud — “B's type, A's palette, C's tab bar, kill D” works perfectly. Every font here is free to bundle in the real app.
