Where he earns his place

  • S1 welcome — the handshake. Full size, once.
  • Working states — parsing, scheduling: he's visibly on the job.
  • Empty states — “no bills yet” feels friendlier from a person.
  • Notification icon — a tiny Ben face on reminders = instant recognition.
  • B2 manual entry — softens the failure: “that one's hard to read.”

Where he shouldn't be

  • Home & Insights — data is the hero; he'd be wallpaper by week two.
  • Bill rows / confirm — never near editable numbers.
  • Paywall price page — a grinning mascot next to a price reads salesy.
  • Repeated anywhere — one Ben per journey, max.

Two honest notes on the artwork

  • Resolved: the v2 flat redraw sits perfectly in Forest Bold — this board shows it live.
  • The brand promised one calm expression — this thumbs-up grin is a celebration pose. Perfect for S8; too loud for a bill that's overdue. Worth a second, calmer pose.
My recommendation: keep him — but as a guest, not a resident. Welcome, working states, empty states, and the notification icon; nowhere else. That way every appearance means something, which is exactly Ben's promise about notifications. If you're in, I'll wire him into those five moments natively and we'll judge on device.
