Thanks for writing.

Ben’s trial and Ben Pro are billed through Apple. To cancel or change renewal:

Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Ben

Refunds are handled by Apple when required by their rules — we can’t reverse a
charge from inside the app.

If something looks wrong with the trial timing, say what you’re seeing on the
paywall and we’ll help interpret it.
