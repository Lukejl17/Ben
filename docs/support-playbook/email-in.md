# Forwarded bill not showing (email-in)

**Tags:** forward, email-in, bills@, not arriving, mailroom

**When it matches:** User forwarded a bill and it never appeared under Needs a look.

**Reply skeleton:**

Thanks for flagging that.

Forwarded bills go to your personal `bills-…@in.benandbill.app` address (in
Settings), not to support@. Check:

1. You forwarded to the exact address shown in Ben → Settings.
2. The message had a PDF (or a clear bill image) — logos and signature images
   are ignored on purpose.
3. Pull to refresh on Home, or open the email-in sheet once so Ben can check.

If it’s still missing, tell us the approx. time you forwarded and the issuer
(no PDF needed) and we’ll trace the mailroom.

— Ben Support
