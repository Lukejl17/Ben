# Tone rules — Ben Support

Same bar as in-app Ben voice.

- Calm, plain, warm Australian tone — not slangy.
- No panic language, guilt, exclamation marks, or urgency theatre.
- Factual: say what we know, what to try, what we cannot see (bills stay on device).
- Never invent a fix, refund, or “we’ve patched that” without evidence.
- Never ask for full bill PDFs, BPAY refs, or passwords in Slack or email unless
  strictly required — prefer issuer + symptom.
- If unsure: say you’ll check with the team and follow up — do not guess policy.
- Sign-off: `— Ben Support` (no confetti, no “Hope that helps!!”).
